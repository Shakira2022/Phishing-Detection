Model scenario (3 marks)
Give the scenario of your model. How is this model useful in the context of

3.2
Model architecture (10 marks)
Explain all relevant details of the model you used. For example, if you imple-
mented a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), specify the types of layers, the
activation functions used, and the overall model architecture. Also explain why
the chosen model is well suited to the data it was trained on.
3.3
Model result analysis (10 marks)
Explain the results of your model using the metrics and graphs derived from the
model’s training. Is this model effective in the problem it is trying to resolve?
3.4
Real-world use of models (7 marks)
Evaluate the real-world use of models within the field of cybersecurity, focusing
on how these models are applied to tasks such as intrusion detection, threat clas-
sification, anomaly detection, and risk assessment. Discuss their effectiveness in
practical deployments, including strengths, limitations, and common challenges
such as data quality, concept drift, and false positives. Use academic references.
